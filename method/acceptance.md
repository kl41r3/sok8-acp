## Inheritance and exception boundaries

Scientific scope, I1/I2 commercial associations, T1 type, T2 cutoff/languages, V1 identity/version, deduplication, evidence preservation and complete retrieval inherit ACM. v2 explicitly adds the future Abstract-field default and targeted full-text evidence path; adapt A0/X2 under workflow Section 3. Separately record missing abstracts, unavailable full text, still-uncertain readings and clearly out-of-scope cases. Exceptions change no other eligibility condition and do not retroactively revise historical ACM with body-text supplements.

Verify platform field capabilities against actual interfaces/documentation; deviation from an unavailable equivalent Abstract field requires an explicit decision. Targeted reading of one record does not authorize database-wide AllField/full-text queries. Text locations, evidence levels and review are necessary for final judgments; successful download is insufficient for acceptance.
