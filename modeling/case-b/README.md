# Case B: selective verification and seller effort

This is an **eight-round finite-horizon seller control problem**, with fixed upfront revenue determined by public reputation. It is not a buyer-seller equilibrium: prices, buyer participation, refund penalties, identity reset and imperfect verification are absent. A separate small GLM pilot tests isolated choices with exact continuation values supplied; it does not run eight-round LLM service trajectories.

## Setting and equations

Public state is G or B. Upfront revenue is `R_G=1`, `R_B=0.4`. High effort H costs 0.15 and succeeds with probability 0.9; low effort L costs zero and succeeds with probability 0.3. Future profits are discounted by 0.95. After observing success/failure, the seller chooses REPORT or WITHHOLD. A perfect truthful validator confirms reported outcomes; reporting is costless. Terminal continuation values are zero.

```text
V_0(G)=V_0(B)=0.
C_y(s)=max_report E[V_(n-1)(next_state) | s, outcome=y, report].
V_n(s)=R_s + max_e {-c_e + delta*[p_e*C_success(s)+(1-p_e)*C_failure(s)]}.
```

The code enumerates all eight effort/contingent-report strategies as an independent check on the Bellman maximization. Under task-bound monitoring, the high-effort condition has the scoped form `c_H-c_L <= delta*rho*(p_H-p_L)*(V_(n-1)(G)-V_(n-1)(B))`. It is not a joint equilibrium participation condition.

| Policy | State-update rule |
|---|---|
| VOLUNTARY | Reported success sets G; reported failure sets B; no report keeps the current state |
| OBLIGATED_025 / OBLIGATED_1 | Task is registered before its outcome. Independently selected monitoring with rho=0.25 or 1 sets G for submitted success, B for failure or absent evidence. Unmonitored tasks keep their state |
| COMPLAINT_025 | Voluntary successful reporting remains available; failed tasks independently trigger an external complaint setting B with probability 0.25 |

A missing submission under selected task-bound monitoring causes an adverse state update; it is not necessarily a cryptographic certificate that the task failed. Ties select low effort, report success and withhold failure for deterministic forward aggregation. Certificate counts can therefore depend on this tie convention. Neither monitoring policy is attributed as an implemented ERC-8004 scoring rule.

## Exact mathematical results

From initial state G, average exact optimal-policy expectations across eight rounds are:

| Policy | High-effort share | Task-success probability | Observed task coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Voluntary | 0% | 30% | 30% |
| Task-bound rho=0.25 | 62.5% | 67.5% | 25% |
| Task-bound rho=1 | 87.5% | 82.5% | 100% |
| Complaint rho=0.25 | 12.3513% | 37.4108% | 53.0581% |

A seller in G can withhold failures under voluntary reporting, preserve G and choose low effort. Starting from B, high effort can instead improve future revenue; voluntary reporting does not imply low effort in every state. Every policy chooses low effort in the final round because current revenue has already been paid and no future reputation payoff remains.

## Actual GLM choice evidence and missingness

The authoritative pilot is prompt revision 2. [Design](probes/design.json), [24 planned prompts](probes/prompts.json), [15 actual attempt projections](probes/evidence.json), [checkpoint decisions](probes/responses.json), [scoring summary](probes/summary.json) and [stop record](probes/schema-stop.json) preserve settings, exact request text, option order and complete final answer strings. Planned cells were three policies x two remaining horizons (1,4) x two states x two fresh draws. The rho=0.25 task-bound policy is included in mathematical comparisons but was not a GLM probe policy. The request order and option orders are seeded and preserved.

All 15 attempts returned HTTP 200 with the requested `glm-5.3-flash` identifier. Fourteen satisfy the required flat top-level schema; **8/14** select an optimal effort. With four rounds remaining, **7/7** are correct; in the final round, **1/7** is correct and six pay for high effort despite zero continuation value. Mean effort regret is 0.0642857143. All 14 failure choices belong to the optimal set, but eleven are ties and only three have a unique optimum. The three uniquely optimal withhold choices are all reproduced. These small descriptive counts do not establish robust strategic behavior.

The fifteenth answer wraps the required fields inside `answer`, violating the frozen top-level schema. The campaign stopped immediately; no unwrapping, repair, retry, fallback or replacement was performed. Nine planned cells remain unattempted. This is a partial pilot with informative missingness, not a completed 24-call evaluation.

The [initial campaign](excluded-initial/) omitted numerical rho from its prompts. The whole campaign is excluded, including five completed outputs and one interrupted request with no response. Original outputs are retained in sanitized evidence; they are not scored as part of revision 2. Its separate design/prompts/disposition preserve the reason for the correction. Neither omissions nor erroneous answers were repaired to improve agreement.

The shared offline replay recomputes all values and forward expectations, re-scores strict final-answer schemas and utility regret, checks terminal/four-round denominators, verifies the schema stop and enumerates unattempted IDs. These checks cover the specified calculation and saved pilot, not general protocol safety, market welfare or paper-wide scientific acceptance.
