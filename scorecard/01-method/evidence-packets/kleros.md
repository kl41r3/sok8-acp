## Failures named in sources

- Original L8 table (instrument, not a score): Kleros latency is **days**; agent-speed = no.
- Subjective quality and off-chain delivery can be judged, but not at machine transaction speed.
- Loss allocation depends on the arbitrable contract (escrow vs token vs registry). Not a native agentic-commerce standard.
- Ledger T6: evidence ≠ remedy unless a forum and consequence are wired. Kleros is a forum, but not wired into AP2/x402/UCP as a default L8.
- `oa-w4412976487` discusses recognition of blockchain awards; cross-jurisdiction enforcement is not automatic.
