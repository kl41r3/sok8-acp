## Documented artifacts

- HTTP 402 Payment Required; client retries with cryptographic payment proof (`PAYMENT-SIGNATURE`).
- No account signup or KYC in the base flow (“Zero friction: No accounts or personal information needed”).
- Blockchain-agnostic: EVM, Solana, Algorand in SDKs; CAIP-2 network IDs; stablecoins primary.
- Schemes: `exact`, `upto` (usage-based, EVM Permit2), `batch-settlement`.
- Facilitator verifies and settles; mainnet uses production facilitators (Coinbase CDP, PayAI); `x402.org/facilitator` is testnet-only.
- Optional Bazaar discovery extension; optional Signed Offers & Receipts (signed offer on 402, receipt on 200) for audit / dispute / reputation.
- IETF drafts for payment evidence frame, refund receipt, cancellation receipt, compliance receipt, PQC credential binding, delegation binding.
- Marketing page (2026-07-14): 75.41M tx / $24.24M vol / 94k buyers / 22k sellers last 30 days; Linux Foundation x402 Foundation; members include Stripe, Cloudflare, AWS, Alchemy.
- Multiple independent SDKs (TS, Go, Python) and middleware (Express, Next, Hono, FastAPI).

## Failures named in sources

- Payment completion is distinct from service delivery (`consensus_dissent_ledger` L5; T3).
- Base protocol has no native subjective-quality forum. Receipts extension is optional.
- Facilitator is a trusted or semi-trusted settlement helper; not a court.
- Corpus companions document attacks: `oa-w7161203592` Five Attacks on x402; `oa-w7163286756` Free-Riding the Agentic Web; refund/compliance still IETF drafts.
- Irreversible on-chain settlement is both finality and a recourse deficit (ledger L5 dissent).

## What is not in the packet

No field measurement of time-to-economic-finality across fiat legs. Marketing volume figures are from the project site, not an independent audit.
